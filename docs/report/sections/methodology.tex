\section{Methodology}\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Timing.} Kernels are timed with CUDA events: 5 warm-up runs, then the median of 20 with the
interquartile range. The 24\,MB L2 cache is flushed before every timed SpMV, because matrices smaller than the cache
would otherwise look faster than they are. Out-of-core SpGEMM is timed on the host from pinned inputs to pinned output,
including every transfer and the estimation and planning time. Pinned allocation and context creation are excluded
from the total and reported separately. Under WSL2, host-side timing shows position effects of 5--10\,\textmu s, which
a fixed order of methods turned into fake differences of $\pm 9\%$ at $N=1$ in Phase~3. Every comparison of
selectors or SpGEMM methods therefore runs the methods in a freshly shuffled order in each repetition.

\paragraph{Correctness.} Every SpMV result is compared with a CPU reference accumulated in double precision
(relative $L_2$ error $\le 10^{-12}$ in fp64, $\le 10^{-5}$ in fp32). Every SpGEMM run must give the same
$\mathrm{nnz}(C)$ as every other method, and 2,000 random rows (plus the first and the last) are recomputed on the CPU.
CG residuals are recomputed on the CPU from the downloaded solution. Each AMG level is checked against the other
methods and against the Galerkin row-sum identity $A_{l+1}\mathbf 1 = R_l(A_l \mathbf 1)$, which holds exactly for
Laplace. All CSVs carry the GPU, driver, CUDA and cuSPARSE versions, the git commit and a dirty flag.

\paragraph{Data.} SpMV: \stat{p2-matrices} SuiteSparse matrices~\cite{davis2011suitesparse} for training (50 drawn at
random, real and square with $10^4$--$5\cdot10^6$ nonzeros, plus 10 named in paper~2) and \stat{p3-heldout-matrices}
different matrices drawn the same way for evaluation, never seen by training. CG: every SPD matrix of these sets plus
20 more SPD matrices, and Laplace3D. SpGEMM: paper~1's multigrid products on the 7-point Laplace3D with $2\times2\times2$
aggregation ($P$, $R=P^T$), generated in memory, which allows multi-GB problems.

\paragraph{Metrics.} SpMV: GFLOP/s ($2\,\mathrm{nnz}$ per call), effective bandwidth as a fraction of the
\emph{measured} \stat{pf-device-read}\,GB/s, and slowdown $T/T_\text{oracle}$ relative to the fastest of all
configurations. Selector comparisons use geometric means and Dolan--Mor\'e performance
profiles~\cite{dolan2002profiles}. SpGEMM: GFLOP/s counts two flops per multiply-add over wall time.

\paragraph{Platform hygiene.} AC power and the Windows ``best performance'' mode throughout. Median timing noise in
the Phase~1 baseline was \stat{p1-iqr-median-pct}\% (IQR over median).
